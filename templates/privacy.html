Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we also store the expense records you create — including amounts, categories, dates, and any notes you add. We do not collect payment information or any sensitive financial account details.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Spendly service. This includes displaying your expense history, generating summaries, and maintaining your account. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. Upon deletion, your data is permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not integrate with or share your data with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Any external libraries used (such as fonts) are loaded from public CDNs and are subject to their own privacy policies.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about how your data is handled, please reach out via the contact information provided in the app. We are committed to addressing your privacy concerns promptly.
